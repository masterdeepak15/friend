# The eye and the people Friend remembers

See the README sections "The eye: letting Friend look by itself" and "People Friend remembers" for the rules in full. This page is the short version.

## In one minute

- Esc → Settings → Approvals → **The eye**: switch it on with your PIN. Friend can then look through the camera by itself, but only when you talk to it at its own screen.
- It switches itself off after the minutes you set, when you sign out and when Friend restarts. Switching off needs no PIN.
- Nothing from a picture is saved. The audit log says that Friend looked, when and how, never what it saw.
- Say "this is Priya, my sister" while she is in front of the camera: Friend asks for your PIN and remembers her face as numbers. Remove one person or everyone in the same Settings page.

## What it can not do

- It can not be switched on by the AI, by Telegram, WhatsApp or Alexa, by a guest or a worker, or by a web page or words inside a picture.
- A face never approves anything. Only your PIN does.

## Limits

20 looks and 30 seconds of video per hour without a card; a clip is at most 10 seconds and 12 pictures. After that Friend asks again.

## Privacy

Pictures go to the model you chose: Anthropic with Claude, nowhere with a local Ollama vision model. Face numbers stay on your PC, encrypted. Introduce only people who know and agree, and not children.
